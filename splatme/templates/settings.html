SplatMe

Trains a task's Gaussian splats on a splat node: a container with a GPU that runs beside WebODM. Training starts from Train Splats in a completed task's panel on the Dashboard, or by itself when a task completes.

Automatic Training

A task can be trained by itself when it completes processing, and again each time it is processed again. Each task has this setting in its panel on the Dashboard (Train Splats When Completed); a task without a setting of its own follows the default here.
